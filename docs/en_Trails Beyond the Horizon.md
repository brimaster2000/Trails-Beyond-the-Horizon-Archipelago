# Trails Beyond the Horizon

## What does randomization do to this game?
Treasure chests (and optionally Grim Garten chests and domain clears) become checks.
Each act route (Act I - A, B and C, the six Act II routes, the six Act III routes and the Finale) is locked behind an item.

## What is the goal?
Defeat the final boss. The final boss door opens once the goal condition chosen in your options is met.
